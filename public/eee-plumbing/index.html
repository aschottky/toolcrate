A letter from the plumber who will actually turn up.

My name is Michael Ellegaard. I learned this trade in Denmark, I have been at it for twenty-eight years, and for the last stretch of them I have been under houses here in the Ozarks. When you call EEE Plumbing, you get me or you get Isaac. That is the whole company.

I want to tell you plainly how we work, because most plumbing websites do not, and I think that is why people dread making the call.

First: we will tell you if you don't need us

The most expensive thing in plumbing is not the part. It is being told you need a big job when you needed a small one. If you already have a quote in your hand and it does not sit right with you, read it to me on the phone. I will tell you honestly whether that is the repair I would do. Sometimes it is. Sometimes it is not.

“Can't recommend this company enough! They saved us $1,600 from an estimate that Roto Rooter gave us — it was a simple fix and we're good to go now! EEE Plumbing is a company with excellent values, morals and ethics.” Kate Gately · Google review

That review is the reason this page exists. A second opinion costs you a phone call.

What we get called out for

Water heaters — repaired, replaced, or swapped to tankless. Drains and sewer lines, including camera work when we need to see what is actually down there. Gas piping, which in Missouri you want a licensed master plumber for. Water line repair and whole-house repipes. Rough-in for remodels, additions and new construction. And the ordinary things: tubs, showers, toilets, fixtures, hardware. If it carries water or gas, it is ours. If it does not, I will tell you who to call instead.

Who comes to the door

Me, or Isaac. Isaac is the name customers keep typing into their reviews, and I understand why — he explains what he found before he touches anything.

Where we go

Springfield is home. From there we run out to Nixa, Ozark, Republic and Clever to the south and west; Rogersville, Fordland, Strafford and Fair Grove to the east and north; and further when it makes sense — Ash Grove, Walnut Grove, Halltown, Billings, Sparta. If your town is not on that list, call anyway and I will tell you honestly whether the drive makes the job worth it for you.

When to call

Monday through Friday, seven in the morning until half past four. Tell me what it is doing and where the water is going — those two facts decide almost everything.
