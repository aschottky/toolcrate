Last Updated: June 29, 2026

ToolCrate (“ToolCrate,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates usetoolcrate.com (the “Site”) and provides AI-powered web design and development services (the “Services”), including our Free Preview, Full Build, and Conversion OS. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who We Are (and Who This Policy Covers)

ToolCrate is operated by Alexander Schottky and primarily serves small business owners in the United States, though visitors may come from other regions.

This Policy covers personal information we collect when you:

  • visit the Site,
  • submit the “Get my free Design Preview” form,
  • purchase Services (including subscriptions),
  • communicate with us, or
  • otherwise interact with ToolCrate.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide, information collected automatically, and information from payment and marketing tools you use with us.

A. Information you provide to us

  • Contact information: Such as your name and email address (especially through the “Get my free Design Preview” form), and any other details you include in messages to us.
  • Business information: Such as your business name, your current website URL, and details you submit that help us generate a preview or deliver Services.
  • Project and communications content: Information you send us about your goals, preferences, feedback, and other project-related details.
  • Account/billing-related information (limited): If you purchase Services, we may collect basic billing metadata (like your plan, invoices, payment status, and transaction IDs). We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

B. Information we collect automatically

  • Device and usage data: Such as IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages viewed, referring/exit pages, approximate location (derived from IP), and timestamps.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: See the Cookies section below.

C. Payment information handled by third parties

When you purchase Services (including $497 + $99/mo for Full Build and $2,500 + $997/mo for Conversion OS, as presented at checkout), your payment is processed by Stripe. Stripe collects and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. ToolCrate generally receives confirmation of payment and limited transaction details—not your full card number.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to run ToolCrate, deliver the Services, and improve them, including to:

  • Deliver the Free Preview: Generate and send your AI design preview.
  • Provide purchased Services: Build, host, maintain, and improve your website or related deliverables.
  • Communicate with you: Respond to inquiries, send project updates, service notices, and billing-related messages.
  • Marketing (optional): Send product updates or offers. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time (e.g., via the unsubscribe link or by contacting us).
  • Analytics and performance: Understand what’s working on the Site and improve user experience, conversions, and content.
  • Security and fraud prevention: Protect the Site, our customers, and ToolCrate.
  • Legal and compliance: Comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information in the following situations:

  • Service providers (processors): Companies that help us operate, such as hosting providers, analytics providers (including Google Analytics), email services (including Resend), payment processing (Stripe), and AI infrastructure partners—only as needed to perform services for us.
  • Payment processors: To process payments and manage subscriptions (ToolCrate does not receive your full payment card number).
  • Business transfers: If we’re involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction (subject to appropriate safeguards).
  • Legal requirements: If required to comply with law, court order, subpoena, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the Site working smoothly, understand traffic, and improve performance. We use Google Analytics (GA4) to measure Site usage.

Types of cookies we may use

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic Site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand usage and improve the Site.
  • Marketing/advertising cookies (if used): Help measure campaigns and improve relevance.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings (block, delete, or limit cookies). If you disable certain cookies, parts of the Site may not work properly. Some analytics providers may offer additional opt-out tools (depending on the provider and your region).

If you’re in the EEA/UK (or where similar laws apply), we process personal information when we have a legal basis, including:

  • Legitimate interests: To operate, secure, and improve ToolCrate (balanced against your rights).
  • Consent: Where required (for example, certain marketing communications or non-essential cookies, depending on your location).

7. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA and GDPR)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information.

A. California (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Access specific pieces of personal information.
  • Delete personal information (subject to certain exceptions).
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (ToolCrate does not sell personal information; if we ever engage in “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined by law, we’ll provide appropriate choices).
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

How to submit a request: Email alexander@usetoolcrate.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

B. EEA/UK (GDPR)

If you’re in the EEA/UK, you may have the right to:

  • Access your information.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your information.
  • Restrict processing.
  • Object to processing.
  • Data portability (receive your data in a usable format).
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
