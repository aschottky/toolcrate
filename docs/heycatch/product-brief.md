# Product brief (HeyCatch, Aug 2026)

Source: HeyCatch Foundation > Product brief. Describes usetoolcrate.com as HeyCatch read it in Aug 2026.

## Geo scope
- URL: https://usetoolcrate.com
- Geo scope (assumed): US. USD pricing ($497, $79/mo), a +1 (818) phone number, US city examples (Austin, St. Louis), and a "Missouri-friendly" AI persona all signal a US market. No non-US currency, VAT, or regional compliance signals.
- Operator: unstated. Site brands itself as "Toolcrate," founded/fronted by "Alexander Schottky." No legal entity, address, or company registration disclosed; only support@usetoolcrate.com and a phone number.

## One-liner and category
ToolCrate is a founder-led, done-for-you web design service that builds conversion-focused websites for high-stakes local service businesses (contractors, heavy trades, and specialized professional firms), competing against local web agencies and DIY builders like Wix.

## Hero (verbatim)
- H1: "I build websites that make the phone ring."
- Sub: "I'm Alexander. I've spent 15 years auditing sites that should be printing money but are just burning it. I don't use generic templates. I personally hand-vet every conversion leak to ensure your phone stays busy."
- CTAs: "Get YOUR Free Website Preview" (primary, to /try/). No distinct secondary CTA; a recurring "Get My Free Roast" variant appears mid-page.

## Pricing
Flat productized pricing, positioned explicitly against agency cost. The funnel opens with a free redesign preview (no card), then a one-time build fee plus a flat monthly care plan. There is no live checkout and no dedicated /pricing page (returns 404); all numbers come from the homepage "Real Results" comparison block:
- Free Website Preview: $0, custom concept designed before you pay. "100% Free. No commitment."
- ToolCrate full build: $497 one-time (vs. quoted "$10,000 - $25,000" agency price).
- Care plan: $79/mo hosting and updates (hosting, SSL, updates, security patches, backups, minor content changes).
- Claimed "You save $9,500+ on day one." The Build carries a 30-day money-back guarantee; a Monday giveaway offers "a FREE Toolcrate site + hosting for a year."

## Features
- Free pre-purchase conversion audit and redesign concept delivered within 48 hours via a "drop your URL" form.
- Conversion architecture: phone number and primary action above the fold, single-goal pages.
- Mobile-first builds with thumb-friendly buttons and fast scroll.
- Speed-focused stack: built on Vite, deployed on edge hosting, no WordPress-plugin bloat or third-party trackers.
- Founder-reviewed builds ("Alexander reviews every one"), no junior/generator hand-off claimed.
- Market-specific design tuned to the client's local competition and vertical.
- Full ownership of design, code, and content; files handed over on exit.

## ICP signals
- Explicit target: "high-stakes service providers": contractors and heavy trades (excavation, HVAC, roofing, plumbing, electrical) as the core, plus legal/medical/consulting where local reputation drives calls.
- Copy speaks to solo operators paying for traffic that bounces, overpaying agencies, or wasting time on DIY Wix, i.e. small local-business owners, not marketing teams.
- Low $497 price point plus phone-call KPIs point to small local service businesses, which sits in tension with the "specialized professional firms" claim (a $497 site is unusually cheap for high-value legal/medical firms).

## Trust spine
- Quantity: animated counters only: "15+ Years Experience," "200+ Sites Launched," "3x Avg. Lead Increase," "48hr Turnaround." No client logo wall, no live customer count. (D3.1)
- Story: none. Zero testimonials, named clients, or case studies; only a self-authored founder quote. (D3.2)
- Transactional: "No card required," "100% Free. No commitment," a 30-day money-back guarantee on The Build, and stated ownership of files. No security/compliance badges and no visible payment marks. (D3.4)
- Third-party: none. No Product Hunt, G2/Trustpilot/Google ratings, press, or awards. (D3.5)
- Founder or team: named founder "Alexander Schottky" with a photo and an About section, framed as direct-access solo operator. But no LinkedIn/X, no last-name-verifiable links, and no company page. (D3.3)
- Missing: no testimonials, no client logos, no case studies, no third-party review ratings, no press, no security/compliance badges, no verifiable founder identity, and no legal operating entity. Every claimed stat is self-reported and unsourced.

## Tone
Blunt, punchy, first-person and founder-voiced, closer to a direct-response copywriter than a corporate SaaS site. It leans on pain-agitation ("digital ghost town," "an expensive brochure nobody reads," "DIY is killing your business") and confident, plain-spoken authority ("A website should earn its keep"). Energy resembles a hard-selling agency/freelancer landing page; the "Rachel" AI-giveaway gimmick and heavy self-reported stats add a slightly gamified, unverified edge.
