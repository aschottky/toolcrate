# Competitors (HeyCatch, Aug 2026)

Source: HeyCatch Foundation > Competitors.

## Summary
- Market landscape: a crowded founder-led-website field for local service trades. Buildrok leads on product maturity (self-serve builder, live editor, Lead Inbox, $29/mo); ServiceBuilt Digital is the closest ICP twin (operator-built trade sites, free redesign, 48hr). HoneyBun and SPDFY flank; agencies and DIY-plus-freelancer are the real incumbents.
- Dominant value prop: everyone sells "agency-quality, not agency prices" for phone-ringing trade sites. Buildrok owns "Agency-quality websites, priced for working trades"; ServiceBuilt owns "Built by the trades, for the trades. Not a template. A weapon."
- Dominant pricing model: free-preview-then-pay is the category norm. Entry points: Buildrok $29/mo DIY, SPDFY $97/mo, ToolCrate $497 + $79/mo, ServiceBuilt $3,500 build, agencies $10K-$25K. Free tier = a preview/mockup, not a product tier.
- Dominant trust mechanic: a named, visible operator plus real case-study numbers (HoneyBun's +243% YoY, ServiceBuilt's named client URLs, Optimized Growth's 80+ reviews). Exactly what ToolCrate lacks.

## Positions to own
1. The done-for-you-at-DIY-price lane: a full human build for $497 undercuts ServiceBuilt's $3,500 while beating Buildrok on "you don't touch it."
2. Verifiable operator proof: real case-study numbers would close the zero-testimonial gap.
3. Speed plus ownership: 48hr turnaround + "you own the files" beats agency timelines and the $10K-$25K agency build.

## ServiceBuilt Digital (closest ICP, direct)
- URL: servicebuiltdigital.com. Conway, AR base, expanding "nationwide, remotely."
- Hero: "Websites for the trades. Built by the trades." / "We build high-converting websites and SEO systems for home service companies. No fluff. No outsourcing."
- Positioning: operator credibility. "We run our own 7-figure service business and build the same systems for you." ICP named explicitly (plumbers, HVAC, electrical, roofing).
- Features: hand-coded WordPress on Kadence; city pages per market; full schema bundle; mobile-first conversion nav; GBP alignment; free homepage mockup.
- Pricing: Full Build $3,500 (50% deposit); SEO and Local Search $650/mo; Full Build + Growth $3,500 + $650/mo. Free mockup as funnel entry; no self-serve checkout.
- Trust: named founder Travis Ford with operator story; named client project URLs (directserviceoverhead.com, apextattoostudio.com); case-study carousel; city pages. No third-party review badges.
- Page structure: hero + free-mockup form, who-we-build-for (12 trades), problem, three tiers, 4-week process, work/case studies, founder, service areas, FAQ, final CTA form.
- Distribution: trade x city landing-page SEO, operator-authority content, free-mockup funnel.
- Better than ToolCrate: concrete proof (client URLs, case studies); 12 dedicated trade pages; credible operator story tied to a real service business.
- Worse than ToolCrate: 7x the entry price; WordPress/Kadence stack vs Vite/edge; 4-week timeline vs 48hr-7-day.

## Buildrok (most successful, direct/adjacent DIY)
- URL: buildrok.com. Astro, Neon, Stripe, Cloudflare.
- Hero: "Trade websites built to book jobs." / "Templates built around how your trade actually books work... preview your whole site free, then publish from $29/month."
- Positioning: trade-shaped self-serve builder. "Not a blank canvas." "Agency-quality websites, priced for working trades."
- Features: per-trade templates; trade-specific qualifying lead forms; Lead Inbox with 5-stage pipeline; live in-browser editor; local SEO baked in; tap-to-call bar; free preview.
- Pricing: free preview forever; Single Page $29/mo ($23 annual); Multi Page $49/mo ($39 annual); extra pages +$20/mo. Hosting/SSL included; domain separate. Pricing in top nav.
- Trust: 30 live demo template URLs, named infra partners, transparent compare tables. No founder story or testimonials.
- Distribution: /compare pages (vs Wix/Squarespace/WordPress), 30 indexable demo subdomains, per-trade and per-city preview URLs, product-led free trial.
- Better than ToolCrate: real inspectable product; transparent self-serve pricing and /compare pages; built-in Lead Inbox as ongoing value.
- Worse than ToolCrate: DIY (customer builds and maintains); template-based per trade; no human conversion review or founder accountability.

## Other rivals
- HoneyBun: programmatic local-SEO page deployment (2-7 days, territory lock). Core outcome is ranking surface area, not a conversion-designed build. $997-$1,099 one-time; two case studies with GSC data.
- SPDFY: $97/mo instant automated launch stack (site + phone + email + SEO). Thin proof; "5-minute automated" promise.
- Optimized Growth: full done-for-you acquisition agency (ads + funnels + site, $2K-$4K setup + retainer). 80+ reviews, "+383% avg booking lift."
- Local agency redesign / DIY builder + freelancer: the real status-quo incumbent ($10K-$25K agency build, or Wix + a freelancer).

## Where ToolCrate can credibly win
1. Done-for-you at a DIY price: between "cheap but you do it" (Buildrok) and "done but expensive" (ServiceBuilt).
2. Speed + full ownership: 48hr-7-day turnaround plus "you own all the files, no hostage situations," already stated on the site.
3. Free preview before payment: matches the category norm and beats SPDFY/agencies that require a call or card.

## Where the website doesn't surface those wins
- Zero client logos, testimonials, or named case studies; "200+ sites / 3x leads" counters float unsupported.
- /pricing and /about routes 404; the price advantage lives only inside the homepage Results block.
- No verifiable operator identity (no LinkedIn, company entity, or address) while every spotlight rival puts a named, provable founder front and center.
